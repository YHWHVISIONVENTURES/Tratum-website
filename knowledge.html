Tratum Knowledge Base

A structured reference for understanding Tratum's company, technology, infrastructure model and financial participation thesis.

Tratum is presented as financial infrastructure technology. Regulated institutions remain responsible for applicable banking, compliance and regulatory obligations.

Tratum in one sentence

Tratum is a financial infrastructure technology company building secure physical and digital infrastructure for regulated institutions.

Core product

The Tratum Node is the company's proprietary financial infrastructure device and secure endpoint.

Core problem

The platform addresses infrastructure constraints affecting financial availability, reach and participation.

Core technology

Hardware, embedded banking software, security, transaction processing, offline capability, synchronization, APIs, settlement and audit.

Core customers

Banks, Payment Service Banks, payment institutions, mobile-money operators, agency-banking networks and enterprise financial institutions, subject to applicable regulatory requirements.

Core thesis

Financial participation is ultimately constrained or enabled by infrastructure. Tratum therefore focuses on building infrastructure that extends regulated financial capability toward the edge.
