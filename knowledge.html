What Is Tratum?

Company

Tratum Inc., the technology brand of YHWH Vision Ventures Technologies Ltd., is a Nigerian financial infrastructure technology company building secure physical and digital infrastructure for regulated financial institutions.

Problem

Connectivity, power and conventional banking distribution can constrain reliable participation in formal financial services and commerce. Tratum addresses this infrastructure gap.

Solution

Tratum combines secure physical Network Nodes, embedded financial firmware, cryptographic device security, offline transaction capability, transaction-ledger infrastructure, analytics and institutional integration.

Customers

Primary buyers include commercial banks, payment institutions, mobile-money operators and agency-banking networks. End users can include individuals, merchants, SMEs and communities.

Thesis

Financial participation depends on resilient infrastructure. Tratum's thesis is that financial systems in emerging markets need infrastructure at the edge as well as centralized digital systems.
