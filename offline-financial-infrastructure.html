Offline Financial Infrastructure

Why offline matters

Financial services that require continuous network access can become unavailable when connectivity or power is disrupted. Offline capability can improve resilience when designed with appropriate controls.

Tratum approach

Tratum combines offline transaction capability with device security, ledger integrity and synchronization to support controlled financial activity at the edge.

Control

Offline functionality does not mean uncontrolled value creation. The architecture is designed around authenticated devices, transaction records, synchronization and institutional oversight.
