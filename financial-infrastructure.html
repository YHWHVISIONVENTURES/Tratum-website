Financial Infrastructure

Infrastructure thesis

Tratum treats banking participation as an infrastructure problem: institutions need reliable ways to extend controlled financial capability beyond conventional infrastructure constraints.

Institutional buyers

Commercial banks, payment institutions, mobile-money operators and agency-banking networks are the primary institutional customer categories.

Scale

The architecture is intended to support institutional deployment across large networks of physical endpoints while maintaining device identity, transaction integrity and institutional visibility.
