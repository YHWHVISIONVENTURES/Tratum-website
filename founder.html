Segun Ademigoke

Founder and technology leader behind Tratum's financial infrastructure thesis.

Tratum is presented as financial infrastructure technology. Regulated institutions remain responsible for applicable banking, compliance and regulatory obligations.

Building from infrastructure

Segun Ademigoke founded Tratum around the thesis that financial participation requires infrastructure capable of reaching the edge of economic activity.

Technology direction

The company's documented engineering direction combines hardware banking platforms, embedded financial software, cryptographic security, offline transaction processing, synchronization and institutional integration.

Institution-first philosophy

The Tratum architecture is deliberately designed to complement existing banking systems. Institutions retain control of customer accounts, deposits, liquidity, settlement, compliance and regulatory obligations.

Commercialization

Tratum's commercialization programme is structured around product engineering, secure provisioning, quality assurance, institutional deployment, technical integration and recurring infrastructure services.

Long-term objective

The company aims to contribute to indigenous financial infrastructure engineering capability in Nigeria through engineering development, technology transfer, manufacturing partnerships and institutional deployment.
